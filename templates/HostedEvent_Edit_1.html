Activity information
Aerosol droplets carry the SARS-CoV-2 virus. Intense activity causes more emission of aerosol droplets, while speech volume leads to a higher concentration of aerosol droplets in exhaled air, e.g., singing and shouting spread COVID-19 more easily than whispering and gentle speech. (Note: 1 quantum = 1 expected transmission)
Vulnerability to COVID-19 increases with age. The age demographic of the event is defined by the age range of most of the guests attending. If there is no clear demographic, then it is cautious to select the age range of the oldest group attending.
Environmental information
For humidity, use the relative humidity forecast for the day of your event. The relative humidity is given as a percentage on many weather sites, e.g. https://www.weather.com.
Expected relative humidity in most places is between 30% and 70%. (Note: relative humidity is different from the % chance of precipitation.)
